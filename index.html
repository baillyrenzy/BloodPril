Five habits worth building

Eat for your heart

Build meals around vegetables, fruit, whole grains, beans, fish and nuts. Cut back on salty processed foods, which are a major source of sodium.

Move most days

Brisk walking, cycling or swimming for around 30 minutes on most days is a common goal. Start where you are and build up slowly.

Protect your sleep

Keep a regular bedtime and wake time, dim the lights in the evening and keep screens out of bed.

Make room for calm

Slow breathing, a short walk outdoors or a few quiet minutes can take the edge off a stressful day.

Watch the numbers

A home blood pressure monitor helps you and your doctor see trends. Measure at the same time each day, seated and rested.

Limit alcohol and skip tobacco

Both can push blood pressure up. Ask your doctor for support if you want to cut down or quit.

Smart swaps in the kitchen

  • Season with herbs, garlic, lemon and spices instead of extra salt
  • Choose fresh or frozen vegetables over salty canned ones, or rinse canned foods
  • Swap chips and packaged snacks for unsalted nuts, fruit or yogurt
  • Check nutrition labels for sodium and compare brands
  • Drink water first, and go easy on sugary and alcoholic drinks

These are general wellness tips. Ask your doctor what targets are right for you.
